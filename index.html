Featured project

Pekugara

Visit pekugara.com

PEKUGARA is a student housing marketplace app built for Zimbabwe's university towns, connecting students with verified landlords near campuses like the University of Zimbabwe, Midlands State University, and NUST. It covers the full rental journey in one app: verified landlord listings with upfront safety disclosures, an AI assistant that recommends housing conversationally, in-app chat between students and landlords, and secure pay-per-unlock booking processed through Pesepay. Built natively for iOS and Android with biometric login, it gives students a safe way to search for housing and landlords a trusted channel to reach thousands of renters directly from their phone.

  • Verified landlord listings with safety details (gated, fenced, security guard, CCTV)
  • AI-powered housing assistant and search
  • In-app chat between students and landlords
  • Secure pay-per-unlock booking via Pesepay
  • Landlord dashboard: tenants, capacity, booking requests
  • Native iOS and Android apps with biometric login
Featured project

Gata Gadgets

Visit gatagadgets.store

GATA GADGETS is a custom retail management platform built for a Zimbabwe-based electronics store, covering the full day-to-day operation in one system: point-of-sale receipts with split payments across cash, bank transfer, Ecocash, and courier credit; real-time inventory with low-stock alerts; a customer loyalty program; staff clock-in, commission, and leave tracking; and downloadable PDF quotations and invoices. Installable as a progressive web app and hosted on a lightweight cloud server, it gives the business owner live sales, profit, and cash-flow visibility from any device.

  • Point-of-sale receipts with split/multiple payment methods
  • Real-time inventory with low-stock alerts
  • Customer loyalty program
  • Staff management: clock-in, commission, leave, attendance
  • Daily/weekly/monthly profit & loss reporting
  • Downloadable PDF quotations and invoices
  • Installable as a mobile/desktop app (PWA)
Featured project

Money Saver Appliances

Visit moneysaverappliances.com

Money Saver Appliances is a full-stack e-commerce storefront built for a family-owned appliance shop in Chattahoochee, FL. It replaces a big-box shopping experience with a fast, local-first site where customers can browse refrigerators, ranges, washers, and dryers, then check out through a real Shopify-backed cart. Built with Next.js and Tailwind, it pairs a modern storefront with practical local-business features like same-day delivery and mobile repair booking, so a small store can compete with national chains online.

  • Full product catalog with search and category filtering
  • Live cart and checkout wired to Shopify's Cart API
  • Product detail pages with image galleries
  • Local same-day delivery and mobile repair service info built in
  • Legal and policy pages (returns, warranty, shipping, privacy) for a compliant storefront
  • Responsive design with motion-based animations throughout
