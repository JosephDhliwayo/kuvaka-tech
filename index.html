How installation works

The Mini needs a clear, unobstructed view of the sky — it talks to satellites, not a mobile tower. Positioning is software-assisted through the Starlink app rather than motorized, so a good first placement matters.

That's where we come in: professional installation from $30, more for roof, pole or difficult-access mounts. See installation pricing →
